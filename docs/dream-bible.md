# Dream Bible

## The central metaphor

The product experience begins with Dream Atlas.

Users do not come to write dreams. They come to grow a living map of dream
memory.

- Every preserved dream becomes a star.
- Every recurring motif can become a path.
- Every stable recurring pattern can become a constellation.
- Dream Cards preserve memories.
- Dream Atlas reveals their relationships.
- AI may offer an optional, playful fairy-tale reflection on recurring motifs.
- AI never presents that reflection as diagnosis, fact, prediction, or advice.
- AI helps people return to their memories with curiosity.

## Emotional direction

The product should feel calm, elegant, intimate, poetic, and premium. It should
feel like entering a night sky—not opening an analytics dashboard.

Use cool silver, pearl, deep navy, and restrained violet. Avoid warm gold and
brown selection states, busy dashboard panels, technical labels in the primary
experience, and decorative elements that compete with the memory itself.

## Language

Prefer:

- preserve, remember, return, trace, echo, motif, path, constellation
- observational descriptions grounded in stored memories
- open questions that leave ownership with the user
- clearly labeled, humorous fairy-tale possibilities built from recurring motifs

Avoid:

- analyze, decode, diagnose, reveal the meaning
- certainty about psychology or symbolism
- claims not supported by preserved data

## Interaction story

The canonical product story is:

```text
Atlas → hover → Dream Memory → constellation → Preserve → Dream Card
→ new star in the Atlas
```

Every feature should strengthen this loop.
