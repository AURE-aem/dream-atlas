# Dream Atlas — Product Vision

Dream Atlas is not a diagnostic dream-decoding product.

It is a living memory system. Users come to grow a map of their dream memory:
every preserved dream becomes a star, every returning motif creates a path,
and recurring patterns gradually become constellations.

## The two pillars

**Dream Cards preserve memories.** They make an individual dream beautiful,
readable, and worth returning to.

**Dream Atlas reveals relationships.** It shows that an image, place, or motif
has returned without assigning it a universal meaning.

## AI's role

AI may:

- shape a poetic title and narration,
- extract concrete motifs,
- describe similarities across memories,
- name a deterministically discovered constellation,
- offer open reflection questions,
- turn deterministically ranked recurring motifs into an optional, humorous
  fairy-tale reflection.

AI must never:

- diagnose the user,
- present a playful interpretation as fact,
- claim a universal symbol meaning,
- predict the future,
- hide uncertainty or fallback behavior.

## Product promise

You do not have to remember everything. Preserve the fragment that stayed, and
the Atlas will help you notice what returns.
